Too Much Monkey Business
By Andrew Zhang
(Abbreviated as MonkeyBusiness)

Gameplay:
2D Platformer, AD to move left/right, W to jump.

When you see a rope, jump onto it, and press E within a certain radius to grab onto it.

When on a rope, press AD to apply force to swing harder.
Press WS to climb up/down, towards/away from the anchor.
When on a rope, press E to release and fly in the direction of swinging.
